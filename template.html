    HISTORY & THE MODEL

    The historical setting

    What this miniature assumes

    People, logistics, moving works, gravity, collisions, and projectiles interact in the model. Landscape and time are compressed. The scene uses original procedural geometry and is not a measured archaeological reconstruction.

    Sources to explore

      MAKE YOURSELF AT HOME

      A little field guide.

      • Explore Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom. Right-drag to pan. Use the four views (keys 1–4) to fly closer. Click anyone to see their job and story.
      • Grab & toss (G) Hold a worker or soldier to lift them. Drag slowly and release to drop; flick to throw. Scroll while holding to change lift height. Escape drops them safely.
      • Shockwave (B) Click the ground to knock nearby people off balance. Their jobs resume after they recover.
      • Crews Workers choose the open part of the works that most needs material, tire and rest, and work a night shift by torchlight. Defenders gather on the threatened wall and shoot at the works. Attackers screen the crews, drill, keep watch, and later form up to advance.
      • Time 1×, 4× and 12× speed up the miniature, and a full day passes in eight minutes at 1×. Space pauses. Real construction took far longer.
      • Advance works Completes the current works so you can watch the later phase: towers and guns, relief armies, sorties, and, where the city fell by assault, a breach. Restart rebuilds this siege only.
      • Progress Each siege saves locally. Closing the page stops time; reopening resumes your world.
