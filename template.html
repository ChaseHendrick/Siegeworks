What this miniature assumes

People, logistics, moving works, gravity, collisions, and projectiles interact in the model. Landscape and time are compressed. The scene uses original procedural geometry and is not a measured archaeological reconstruction.

    MAKE YOURSELF AT HOME

    A little field guide.

    • Explore Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom. Right-drag to pan. Use the four camera views to get closer.
    • Grab & toss Hold a worker or soldier to lift them. Drag slowly and release to drop; flick to throw. Scroll while holding to change lift height. Escape drops them safely.
    • Shockwave Click the ground to knock nearby people off balance. Their jobs resume after they recover.
    • Time 1×, 4×, and 12× speed up the miniature. Space pauses. Physics steps stay bounded. Real construction took far longer.
    • Advance works Complete the current earthworks to see the tower, relief patrols, or artillery phase. Restart rebuilds this siege only.
    • Progress Each siege saves locally. Closing the page stops time; reopening resumes your world. Saves are tied to this browser.
